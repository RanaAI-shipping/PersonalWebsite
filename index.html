Muthoot FinCorp ONE · Platform

Turning 3,700 branches from a rival channel into a distribution engine

Problem
After the app launched, branch staff saw it as competition and avoided booking through it. Digital products were stuck at digital-only reach.
Decision
Instead of fighting the channel, I conceived and shipped branch-and-employee attribution on the key app journeys, so branches got credit and incentives for business done through the app.
Outcome
95% of the app's ₹130 Cr NCD raise was branch-assisted. The same "phygital" model now supports other products on the app.

How I lead

Operating principles

  1. Outcomes over output

    My team tracks business metrics, not features shipped. Every PM owns a number, and brings proposed solutions, not just blockers.

  2. Architecture over tasks

    I look for leverage first: platform reuse, delegation and systemic fixes. A backend-driven component that removes a recurring bottleneck beats ten one-off tickets.

  3. Work with the channel you have

    In Indian fintech, trust often lives in a branch or a field agent. The best digital products give the existing network a reason to push them.

  4. Stay hands-on with the tools

    I use Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini daily in product work, and I ship small things myself, so my calls on AI products come from building, not reading.
